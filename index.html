About Department

Department of economics is one of the institution's core humanities and social sciences departments, tracing its origins back to the foundational growth of the college after its establishment in 1961 by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) Trust

The three year Honours programme in Economics of Sri Venkateswara College is one of the most sought after course in University of Delhi. The well-structured programme empowers the student with the skills and knowledge leading to enhanced career opportunities in economics
